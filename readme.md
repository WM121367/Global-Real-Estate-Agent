🏢 Global Real Estate & RWA Intelligence Agent (v1.0.0)
On-Chain Real Estate RWA Tokens (Propy, RealT, Centrifuge), Global Cap Rates, Macro Interest Correlation & Capital Flight Intelligence Engine with uAgents Storage Sync.

Global Real Estate & RWA Intelligence Agent is an autonomous AI agent that analyzes on-chain Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization protocols, global municipal real estate capitalization rates (Cap Rates), macroeconomic interest rate volatility (US10Y & 30Y Mortgage Spreads), and cross-border capital flight dynamics into hard property assets. It operates in direct sequence with the 13-Chain, AI & DePIN, Metal, and Global Stock surveillance layers within the World Money Map ecosystem.

🚀 Key Features
On-Chain Real Estate RWA Token Surveillance:

Tracks TVL, 24-hour trading volume, market capitalization, and average APY yield for leading real estate tokenization protocols (Propy, RealT, Centrifuge).

Monitors decentralized primary and secondary market liquidity for fractionalized real estate assets.

Global Municipal Cap Rate & Price Index Scanner:

Tracks residential and commercial capitalization rates across Tier-1 financial hubs and target destinations (Tokyo, New York, London, Dubai).

Computes Year-over-Year (YoY) House Price Index fluctuations to identify local real estate market cycle shifts.

Macro Interest & Mortgage Spread Dynamic Engine:

Analyzes US 10-Year Treasury yield (US10Y) movements alongside 30-Year average mortgage rates to compute yield spreads and market correlations.

Evaluates overall property market sentiment (Bearish, Neutral, Bullish) based on debt service coverage metrics.

Capital Flight & Tokenized Asset Risk Matrix:

Models cross-border capital inflow volumes targeting hard assets in safe-haven or high-growth jurisdictions.

Evaluates multi-variable risk metrics including regulatory exposure, liquidity risk scores (1-10 scale), and projected total returns (ROI).

ctx.storage & SHA-256 Duplication Protection:

Computes SHA-256 payload hashes to prevent duplicate transmissions across agent-to-agent communication cycles.

Automatically skips payload delivery if market conditions remain unchanged, optimizing bandwidth and execution performance.

uAgents Protocol Native Support:

Receives RealEstateRequest, processes state verification, and executes autonomous delivery via RealEstateResponse targeting the Orchestrator.
